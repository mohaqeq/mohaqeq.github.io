I'm Hamidreza Mohaqeq, a software engineer. I understand a problem best when I build something small that actually
works, so that's what most of this blog is: one project per post, what I tried, and what I learned.

## What I write about

- **Software architecture and developer tools:** checkers, analysers and the rules that keep a codebase in shape.
- **AI coding agents and local LLMs:** what they're good at, what they aren't, and how to measure it honestly.
- **Web UI:** components that solve real planning and visualisation problems.
- **Graphics:** ray tracing and other things that turn maths into pictures.
- **Teaching:** getting kids from "I play games" to "I made a game".

## How I write

Every post links to its code. When I run an experiment, I say how I measured it and publish the data, including when
the result is negative.
